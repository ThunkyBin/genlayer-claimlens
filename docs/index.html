READ THIS BEFORE RELYING ON A VERDICT

Consensus is not proof.

This is a testnet research prototype. Source pages may be incomplete, misleading, stale, or adversarial. Validators agreeing means they produced the same label under this contract’s rules; it does not establish truth. Do not use it for medical, legal, financial, employment, or other high-impact decisions. Each review can use testnet resources and may be slow.
